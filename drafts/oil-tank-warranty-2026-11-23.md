# Oil Tank Warranty: What It Covers, What Voids It, and How Much Weight to Give It

An oil tank warranty is a promise about the tank, and a narrow one. Read the actual certificates from Granby and Roth and the pattern is the same: the maker will stand behind a leak caused by a defect in materials or workmanship, for a stated number of years, as long as the tank was installed the way the maker requires and stays where it was installed. It doesn't cover rust on a steel tank, a bad install, an overfill, or most of what sits around the tank.

That makes the number of years on the box a poor way to pick a tank, and the conditions on the certificate a good way to protect the warranty you do get. This guide goes through what the certificates from two makers sold in our area say, what voids the cover, what happens when the house sells, and when a warranty should and shouldn't drive the decision. A note on whose terms these are. Every warranty below is between you and the manufacturer. We're quoting what the documents say so you can hold them up against your own quote. The certificate for your exact tank is the one that governs.

## What a tank warranty usually covers

Strip away the legal capitals and a tank warranty says this: if the tank leaks because it was built badly, the maker will repair it, replace it, or credit you toward a replacement. Granby, the Quebec maker of the steel and fibreglass tanks you'll see across Southeast New Brunswick, says on its [warranty page](https://www.granbyindustries.com/warranties/) that its products are manufactured to recognized industry standards and are free from defects in materials and workmanship. The page also says the official document for your tank's model, certification, country and installation date is what controls.

Two things to keep in mind from the start.

**The remedy is the tank, not the job.** Granby's page says labour, freight, handling, removal and reinstallation are generally not covered unless the document says otherwise. Replacing a tank in a basement is mostly labour and old-tank removal, so a warranty that hands you a free tank can still leave most of the bill with you. Our [oil tank replacement cost](/oil-tank-replacement-cost/) page shows how the installed price breaks down between tank and work.

**The warranty is about leaks from defects.** It isn't a maintenance plan and it isn't an insurance policy. If the tank is fine but the oil line, the filter or the fill pipe fails, those parts are usually outside it.

## What Granby's steel tank certificate says

Granby's [limited warranty certificate for ULC-S602 steel tanks](https://www.granbyindustries.com/app/uploads/2026/04/WARR.034.CE_.0423_Granby-ULC-S602-Warranty.pdf) applies to tanks manufactured, sold and installed in Canada after 31 March 2023. The points that matter for a homeowner:

| Tank | Leak from a material or workmanship defect |
| --- | --- |
| EcoGard double-bottom steel, indoors or outdoors | 25 years |
| Single-wall obround steel, indoors | 10 years |
| Cylindrical double wall steel (two versions) | 10 years |

The notes under that table are the part to read twice. They say that any leakage or damage caused by corrosion perforating the tank steel, inside or out, isn't covered. That's the way most steel tanks fail, so the 25 years describes a defect in a new tank, not 25 years of protection against rust.

The certificate also lists conditions. It applies only if the tank is installed by an installer whose competency is recognized by the local authorities, and installed, used, serviced and maintained to Granby's guidelines and the applicable codes. It excludes damage from accidents, floods, the transfer of oil from an old tank into the new one, overfill spills, using accessories that aren't ULC-listed or CSA B139 compliant, failing to maintain the tank, and faulty installation. And it says the tank must never have been taken out of service, removed from its original address and reinstalled somewhere else.

Granby doesn't ask you to register. The warranty page says every tank is covered automatically from the installation date. What it does ask is that you can prove the date: without original records, the certificate treats the installation as having happened no more than 180 days after the tank was made. So the installer's invoice matters, and our [paperwork after a new oil tank installation](/blog/new-oil-tank-paperwork/) guide lists what to keep.

Granby's fibreglass and 2-in-1 lines have their own certificates, so check the one that ships with your tank. Our [Granby oil tank guide](/blog/granby-oil-tank/) summarizes the lineup.

## What Roth's certificate says

Roth's [limited warranty certificate](https://www.roth-america.com/app/uploads/2021/02/Warranty-Roth-DWT-Heating.pdf) for its Eco DWT plus 3 double-wall tank says it's valid for tanks installed in the United States and Canada on or after 1 January 2012. It's a longer and more detailed warranty than the steel one, so it also has more ways to lose it.

What it states:

- **Length:** 30 years against leaks caused by defects in materials and workmanship, subject to ordinary wear and tear.
- **Labour:** Roth's certificate says that for the first 10 years Roth pays labour for the repair or replacement, up to a maximum of US$1,000 per case.
- **Property damage:** Roth's certificate says that for the first 10 years Roth reimburses damage to your property from a leak out of a defective tank, including on-site environmental cleanup, up to a maximum of US$2 million (Roth's own figure). It excludes contamination of adjoining land or groundwater.
- **Not covered:** the fill, vent and return system, multiple-tank connections and other accessories.

Those are the maker's figures in US dollars, and they apply only to a leak from a defective tank. It's useful to know the cleanup cover exists. It isn't a substitute for the tank being installed right and kept in good shape, and for an on-site spill from any other cause your home insurance is still the document that matters. ## What voids a tank warranty

Between the two certificates, these are the things that end the cover.

### Installation by the wrong person or the wrong method

Roth's certificate says its obligations don't apply if the tank was installed or repaired by anyone other than Roth or an authorized Roth installer, and the warranty is conditional on installation by an installer Roth certifies (its own term, "Roth Certified Installer"). Granby's requires an installer recognized by local authorities and installation to its guidelines. If the quote you're looking at involves either brand, ask the installer in writing whether their installation meets the maker's warranty conditions. That's a fair question to any contractor.

### Moving the tank

Roth's certificate says the warranty is void if the tank is moved from the place of original installation. Granby's says it applies only while the tank stays at its original installation address, and not to a tank removed and reinstalled at another one. Our guide to [moving an oil tank](/blog/moving-an-oil-tank/) covers the practical side. If you're thinking of shifting a tank a few metres to clear a window or a walkway, the warranty is one more reason to plan the position once, at install.

### Missing paperwork and deadlines

Roth's certificate says the completed certificate must be returned within 30 days of installation, and that failing to submit it voids the warranty. It also says that if you find a leak, you have to call Roth's customer line and notify Roth in writing within 10 days after the leak is discovered or reasonably should have been discovered, and that missing that deadline voids the warranty. Granby needs no registration, but needs proof of the installation date.

### Overfill, neglect and non-approved parts

Both certificates exclude spills from overfills. Roth excludes improper or insufficient maintenance and parts from sources other than Roth or its authorized dealers. Granby excludes accessories that aren't ULC-listed or CSA B139 compliant, and failure to properly maintain the tank. CSA B139 is the installation code those standards come from.

## What happens to a warranty when the house sells

Both certificates say the warranty continues for later owners, with the same limits. Granby's says it applies to the original purchaser and subsequent transferees, but only while the tank remains at the original installation address and only if installed in Canada. Roth's says the warranty remains for the original purchaser and subsequent transferees, but only while the tank remains at the site of original installation.

Granby's warranty page is a little more cautious than the certificate. Its FAQ says warranties generally apply to the original installation location and may not be transferable. So don't tell a buyer the warranty transfers until you've read the certificate for that tank and, if it matters, asked the maker.

What does carry over, and what a buyer's insurer will ask for, is the paper: the installer's invoice, a photo of the label, and the certificate. A tank with that folder is easy for a buyer's insurer to say yes to. A tank without it is a question mark whatever the warranty says.

## How much should warranty length drive the decision?

Some, but less than the number suggests.

**Steel vs fibreglass vs double-wall.** A longer warranty usually goes with a tank that doesn't rust from the inside: fibreglass and polyethylene-lined double-wall tanks. That is a real reason to prefer them in places where steel struggles, such as a damp basement or an outdoor spot in salt air near Shediac. Our [steel vs fibreglass oil tank](/blog/steel-vs-fiberglass-oil-tank/) guide has the comparison, and our [Granby vs Roth](/blog/granby-vs-roth-oil-tanks/) guide covers the two brands head to head.

**But years are not service life.** A 25 year warranty on a steel tank, with corrosion excluded, is not a promise the tank lasts 25 years. And a 30 year warranty doesn't help you if the tank was moved, or the paperwork never went in, or the leak came from a fitting.

**What matters more than the number:**

1. Who installs it and whether that meets the maker's conditions.
2. Where it sits, because you want to place it once.
3. The paperwork, kept in one folder from the day it goes in.
4. Whether it meets what your insurer wants. Some insurers ask about double-wall tanks or tank age regardless of what a warranty says.

So treat the warranty as a tiebreaker between tanks that otherwise suit the house, not as the reason to pick one.

## The decision: what to do with the warranty in front of you

- **You have a new quote.** Ask which tank, which certificate, who installs it and whether that meets the maker's conditions. Get that in the written estimate.
- **You have an existing tank.** Find the label, the invoice and any certificate. If the tank is young and the paperwork exists, you may still be inside a warranty for a defect. If the label is gone or the tank is old, assume you have none.
- **The tank is leaking.** A warranty claim and a spill response are separate things. Deal with the oil first, then the claim. Roth's notice window is 10 days, so don't let a claim sit.
- **You moved the tank or let the install slide.** Check the certificate. If it's void, it's void, and the plan is a sound tank in a good position with clean paperwork from here on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does an oil tank warranty cover rust?

Not on a steel tank. Granby's steel tank certificate says leakage or damage caused by corrosion perforating the steel, inside or out, isn't covered. Warranties on tanks with a non-rusting inner tank are different in kind, but they still cover defects, not neglect. Read the certificate for your exact model.

### Do I have to register my tank to keep the warranty?

It depends on the maker. Granby says no registration is needed and coverage starts automatically on the installation date, though you need proof of that date. Roth's certificate says the completed form must go back within 30 days of installation, or the warranty is void. Check which applies to your tank before the installer leaves.

### Does the warranty transfer when I sell the house?

Both certificates we read say it passes to later owners, but only while the tank stays where it was installed. Granby's website adds that warranties may not be transferable, so confirm with the maker for your model. Either way, hand the buyer the invoice, the label photo and the certificate.

### Does moving the tank void the warranty?

Yes, under both certificates. Roth's says the warranty is void if the tank is moved from the place of original installation. Granby's applies only at the original installation address. Decide the position once, when the tank goes in, and keep the clearances in mind.

### Does the warranty pay for cleanup if my tank leaks?

Only in narrow cases. Roth's certificate includes property damage and on-site cleanup cover up to US$2 million for the first 10 years, if the leak comes from a defective tank, and it excludes adjoining land and groundwater. The Granby steel certificate we read doesn't mention cleanup. Your home insurance is the main protection for spills.
